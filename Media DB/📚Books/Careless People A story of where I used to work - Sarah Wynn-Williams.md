#### 1.1.1.2 Communities
>“I watch the aftermath of the earthquake play out on Facebook. People use the platform to let friends and family know they are safe, share information on road closures, coordinate sharing supplies, post warnings about drinking water, offer tips for managing liquefaction—a new concept to me that is basically the ground melting—offer up spare rooms, and support those who had experienced the unthinkable. The community whose physical infrastructure was damaged found a new infrastructure online.” 

This made me think that yes, we work in a tech company and for most of the year that remains our focus, but it's not the tech that makes it successful, it's people. Reading this took me back to Social network where the initial thought was so crude and direct around dating people and making friends. but things evolve and that's the beauty of society. It can take a technical marvel and throw it away, or it take a small, stupid, simply and turn it something much bigger, so much that it is responsible for elections or political disruption. so, ig it's nice to think about the work we do through people every once in a while. who are we helping, what impact do we bring in their lives, and if it helps someone, it's worth building.

I have pitched ideas which just make people happy, and not even customers always, just consultants, bcz i feel that builds relationships and it matters some where down the line - but sadly most of these do not fly with management.

#### 1.1.1.5 Working hard is cool
> “The other currency that flows through Facebook is stamina. Marne, Elliot, and Sheryl ruthlessly manage their own labor, extracting as much work out of each day as humanly possible. ”

>“When one of the few older women on the team tells me I’m spending too much time at work and this is just a job, I honestly think she’s the one who doesn’t get it.”

Working hard and passion is cool. I feel infuriated when that is ridiculed, and it happens so often. Maybe this is an Indian thing - but it really should be the opposite. We are hungry to make our mark, we should be appreciating hard work. How many people know days when u sleep late or stay back at office. Do they not feel part of the mission? Do they not feel privileged to work at the company they once wanted to join.
