# 1 Quotes
1. It is my feeling that when people give you advice, they are talking to themselves in the past
2. Everything that needs to be said has already been said. But since no one is listening, everything must be said again.
3. Nothing is more important than an unread library.
4. Dont worry about doing research. Just search.
5. Those who do not want to imitate anything, produce nothing.   -Salvador Dali
6. If you have one person you're influenced by, everyone will say you're the next whoever. But if you rip off a hundred people, everyone will say you're so original! -Gary Panter
7. Don't just steal the style, steal the thinking behind the style. You dont want to look like your heroes, you want to see like your heroes.
8. Go make that stuff. `Make the sequel you wanted to see. What could be that alternate ending? What would you rather have that music album sound like. What tool did you find missing in your day-to-day work? Make one and show it to the world. Good things happen because some idiot decided to make an insignificant piece of art.`
9. `Surround yourself with references of things you want to be. Save a ton of pins on Pinterest, and a ton of posts on IG. Make your desk full of artwork and paperwork and whatever it is that you like.`
10. Work that only comes from the head isnt any good. Watch a great musician play a show. Watch a great leader give a speech. You'll see what I mean.  You know the phrase going through the motions? Thats whats so great about creative work. If we just start going through the motions; if we strum a guitar, or shuffle sticky notes around a conference table, or start kneading clay, the motion kickstarts out brain into thinking. `Cmon, sticky notes are free. Just put everything on sticky notes, find patterns over time and sort them, and let your ideas take shape.`
11. It's so important to have a hobby. A hobby is something creative that's just for you. You dont try to make money or get famous off it, you just do it because it makes you happy. A hobby is something that gives but doesnt take.
12. When we get home, home is still the same. But something in our minds has changed, and that changes everything.
