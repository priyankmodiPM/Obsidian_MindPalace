# 3 Review
This book is basically a short version of Hamlet for me.
My physics professor always used to say that one of the mistakes of the human race was assigning names or words for everything, and in the process demeaning their impact. Take, pain for example, or love, or hate; these are very strong experiences and yet we capsule every time this series of thoughts that has gone through our heads, into a single word.

Dostoyevsky, though, shows us that words can in fact be used well, and rather creatively to communicate our exact feelings. And just for that, i give this a 3.5/5. 

The story itself, is rather forcefully dramatic. I read a few reviews like "the intense ramblings of a virgin" and "depressed man gets friend zoned twice." that kind of aptly summarise it, a bit brutally if i'm being honest. Maybe, romance is dead, that i find this appropriate. 

Anyways, the story arc itself, i couldn't care less about. It's very simple, something a lot of us might have experienced at some point in our lives, definitely not as strongly as the narrator does, but at least partially. It simply teaches us to manage expectations and the fact that we cannot control how people feel about us. That part has always been quite clear to me, and maybe that's why this didn't impact me that heavily. Apart from that, nothing creatively interesting from a story pov.
